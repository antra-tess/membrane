- A 400 that refuses the account rather than the request is an `auth` error, keeping `httpStatus: 400` and its `providerErrorCode`, as a 402 already is. Anthropic sends two such refusals as `invalid_request_error`, with no code of their own: "Your credit balance is too low to access the Anthropic API", and "You have reached your specified API usage limits. You will regain access on …". Both used to be `invalid_request`, which agent-framework's poison-history breaker reads as a request worth shedding history over. They are read inside OpenRouter's wrapper too.
- A Gemini 429 `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` is no longer retryable when its `QuotaFailure` names a quota no retry can outlast: one with no allocation at all (`quotaValue: "0"`), or a daily window (a `quotaId` naming `PerDay` or `Daily`). A per-minute quota stays retryable, and the body's `retryDelay` is still reported either way. A Google quota inside OpenRouter's wrapper is left retryable, since it may be the router's own, which another route can serve.
