- The Anthropic cache keepalive honours a provider's stated wait. It replays recorded requests on its own timer, so a caller that holds its own calls to a model for a `retry-after` (agent-framework's provider waits) could not stop a poke to that model inside the wait. Now, when any call to a model states a wait (a foreground `complete`/`stream` failure carrying `retryAfterMs`, or a refused poke), no lineage of that model is poked until the wait passes. That covers lineages other than the one that failed, including one already due in the same tick.
  - Holds reduce by maximum: a later, shorter wait never shortens one.
  - A wait that cannot be held as an instant (not finite, or past the last instant a Date can represent) holds until the keepalive stops; that is its whole lifecycle, since the keepalive is an optional optimisation. Only the passing of a wait ends a hold. A caller's release of its own admission, such as agent-framework's agent-scoped `release-provider-wait`, reopens that caller's foreground calls but does not clear the model-wide background hold.
  - A refused poke that the adapter classifies as retryable and that states a wait sets the hold and does not count toward `maxConsecutiveErrors`; the count stays where it was and is not reset. Only a wait a hold accepts exempts it: a negative or NaN hint states no wait, so such a refusal holds nothing and counts. A refusal that is not retryable still counts toward the breaker, whatever wait it states, and its wait still holds the model. Before, a poke refused with a wait was one error toward a breaker whose `stop()` is permanent, so three rate-limit episodes could disable the keepalive for the adapter's life.
  - A new `held` keepalive event (`{ type: 'held', model, until, reason }`, `until` null for a hold until stop) reports each hold when it is set. `CacheKeepalive` takes an optional third constructor argument that reads a failure's stated wait and retryability. The Anthropic adapter passes its own error classification, and poke receipts still carry the SDK's error.
