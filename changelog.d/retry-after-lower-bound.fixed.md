- A provider's `retry-after` is now a lower bound inside one call too. A stated wait that fits the schedule's `maxRetryDelayMs` is honored exactly, as before. A longer one, or one that is not a usable non-negative finite number, is no longer clamped to `maxRetryDelayMs` and retried early: the call ends and the classified error carries `retryAfterMs` intact, so the caller can pace the next call. This holds on `complete()` and on the callback `stream()` pre-emission retry, for the base and the overload schedules. Previously a 120 s `retry-after` under the default 30 s cap was retried after 30 s.
- Retry waits longer than one timer can represent (2^31-1 ms, about 24.8 days, reachable only through a configured `maxRetryDelayMs`) are taken in steps instead of firing almost at once, and remain abort-aware.
