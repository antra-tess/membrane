- A `MembraneError` no longer carries a provider's whole error body. Some providers echo the entire rejected request in their error response (one OpenAI-compatible 400 returned ~1.7 MB), and that text used to become the error's `message`, its stack and its `rawError`, and so every log line, `onError` payload and agent-facing failure notice built from the error. The constructor now bounds what every adapter, SSE error frame, SDK error and membrane rethrow passes through it:
  - `message` (and therefore `stack`) to 2,000 characters: its head and tail around a marker stating how many of how many characters were omitted. A message within the bound is unchanged, and bounding is idempotent.
  - `rawError` to 16 KiB of JSON, measured in aggregate (nested bodies, headers and enumerable fields all count). A value within the bound is unchanged. Beyond it, a summary replaces it: `{ truncated, chars | jsonChars, sha256, head }`, so an identical body stays recognizable by its hash without being kept. A cyclic value becomes its acyclic JSON form, and a value that cannot be serialized becomes a short description instead of breaking the error path.
  - `providerErrorCode` to 128 characters. Longer, provider-supplied codes keep their first 64 characters and their length.
  - `serializeError` bounds the `message` and `stack` it copies.
- `type`, `retryable`, `httpStatus`, `retryAfterMs` and the classification itself are decided before construction and are unchanged. Retry counts, the overload schedule and the stream abort disposition decide exactly as they do for the same failure with a small body (regressions with ~1 MB echoes containing "rate", "529", "overloaded_error", "abort" and "aborted"). This holds because #56 decides those by status and by type; on a tree without #56 those consumers search the message text itself.
- Verified with scripted fetch, SDK, Bedrock and SSE responses only; no live provider calls.
