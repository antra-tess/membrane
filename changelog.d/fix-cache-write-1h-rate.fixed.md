- Cache writes made with a 1-hour TTL are now priced at Anthropic's 1-hour rate (2× input) instead of the 5-minute rate (1.25× input). The adapter carries the provider's `cache_creation.ephemeral_1h_input_tokens` split into `usage.cacheCreation1hTokens` (streaming and non-streaming), `calculateCost` prices that share at the new `ModelPricing.cacheWrite1hPerMillion`, and every built-in Anthropic row carries it. Rows without the field, and usage without the split, are priced as before.
