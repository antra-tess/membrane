- Disclose the provider's verbatim terminal token as `details.stop.providerReason` across complete, callback-stream, and yielding responses, independently of the normalized `stopReason` (for example, OpenAI `length` and Gemini `MAX_TOKENS`). Built-in message adapters report this through the optional `ProviderResponse.providerStopReason`; Responses uses its incomplete reason or terminal status. Custom adapters supply that field when they have the raw token. Absent metadata stays absent, including a bare `[DONE]` or locally detected completions EOT marker; Membrane does not guess from a normalized reason. When the adapter's own end-of-turn token ends an `openai-completions` stream, the normalized reason stays `end_turn`, while a `finish_reason` and usage that arrived in that same frame are kept; later frames are not read. Existing adapter-level unknown reasons still log when normalized to `end_turn`.
- `openai-completions` maps `content_filter` to `refusal`, matching its three sibling OpenAI-family adapters.
- Gemini maps the safety enums it previously dropped (`PROHIBITED_CONTENT`, `BLOCKLIST`, `SPII`, `IMAGE_SAFETY`) to `refusal`, passes reasons with no membrane equivalent (`MALFORMED_FUNCTION_CALL`, `LANGUAGE`, `OTHER`) through as the provider token, and surfaces a prompt blocked before generation (`promptFeedback.blockReason` with no candidates) as a refusal instead of an empty clean finish — on `stream()` as well as `complete()`, where a promptFeedback-only frame used to end the stream on the parser's initial `STOP` and reach callers as a clean `end_turn`; both paths now resolve the stop reason through one shared helper, so they cannot drift apart again.
