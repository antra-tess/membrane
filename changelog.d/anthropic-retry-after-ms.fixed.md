- `AnthropicAdapter` reads a provider's stated wait from `retry-after-ms` as well as `retry-after`, and prefers `retry-after-ms`, the order `@anthropic-ai/sdk` reads them in. With the SDK's own retries off, the adapter's reader is the only one, and it read only `retry-after`. A 429 or 529 that stated its wait only in milliseconds was therefore retried on Membrane's own backoff, inside the wait, and its error carried no `retryAfterMs` for the caller to pace by. A `retry-after-ms` of zero is a stated wait of zero, and so is a negative one, which is already over (as `@anthropic-ai/sdk` treats it). A value that is not a finite number is ignored, and `retry-after` states the wait instead.
  - Verified with scripted fetch through the real SDK on a fake clock, on `complete()` and callback `stream()`; no live provider calls.
