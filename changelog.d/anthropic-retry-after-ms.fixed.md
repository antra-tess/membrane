- `AnthropicAdapter` reads a provider's stated wait from `retry-after-ms` as well as `retry-after`, and prefers `retry-after-ms`, the order `@anthropic-ai/sdk` reads them in. The adapter used to read only `retry-after`, so a 429 or 529 that stated its wait only in milliseconds reached Membrane, and the caller, with no `retryAfterMs`. The SDK's own retries read `retry-after-ms`, honouring a wait under 60 s; with them off (see the entry on the SDK's retries), this reader is the only one, and Membrane's retries and the caller's pacing both follow it. A `retry-after-ms` of zero is a stated wait of zero; the SDK reads `retry-after` instead when `retry-after-ms` is zero, and this reader does not. A negative one is a wait that is already over, so it is also zero (the SDK replaced one with its own backoff). A value that is not a finite number is ignored, and `retry-after` states the wait instead.
  - Verified with scripted fetch through the real SDK on a fake clock, on `complete()` and callback `stream()`; no live provider calls.
