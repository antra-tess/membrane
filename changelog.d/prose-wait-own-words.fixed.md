- A wait stated in prose ("retry after N seconds") is read only from the provider's own words: the message a body gives, or the caller's message when there is no body or it is `null`. Before, it was read from the caller's message whenever there was one, and otherwise from the body's message or a rendering of the body, and an SDK's rendering of a body that echoes the request could set the wait from the request's text: a 429 whose own message said "Too many requests" waited 600 s because its echoed request said "retry after 600 seconds".
