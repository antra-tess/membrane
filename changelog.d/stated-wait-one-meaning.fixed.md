- Every adapter reads a provider's stated wait from `retry-after-ms` before `retry-after` at the shared HTTP boundary, as `AnthropicAdapter` already did, through one exported reader, `statedWaitFromHeaders`. A fetch-based adapter whose provider stated its wait only in milliseconds used to retry on Membrane's own backoff, inside the wait.
- A stated wait below zero, or a `retry-after` date already past, is a wait that is over (0), wherever it is read: a header, an error body (`retry_after_ms`, `retry_after`, Google's `retryDelay`) or an SSE error frame. A 429 stating `retry_after_ms: -1000` retries on the backoff, as it did before stated waits could end a call. Only a wait that is not a finite number, or one beyond `maxRetryDelayMs`, ends the call with `retryAfterMs` intact.
  - Verified with scripted fetch through the real adapters; no live provider calls.
