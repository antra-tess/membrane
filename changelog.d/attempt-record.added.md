- **`MembraneConfig.onAttempt`**, optional: called with one `AttemptRecord` per provider attempt (a first send, a retry after a failure, a refusal re-issue), on `complete()`, `stream()` and `streamYielding()`, once the adapter's call settles. A record carries the call's `purpose` and `requestId` (as `callerRequestId`), the round and attempt, the provider and model, when the request was sent, the times from then to the first event, the first content, the last event and the end, the longest gaps between events overall and after content, the `max_tokens` sent, the usage, and the ending: the stop reason, with the provider's own stop token and a refusal's category, or the error's type, HTTP status, provider code, whether the caller cancelled and whether a deadline ended it, and for a 400 its template and field path. What it throws or rejects with is ignored. An attempt whose adapter never reported its request through `onRequest` gives no record.
- **`purpose`**, optional, on `complete()`, `stream()` and `streamYielding()`: `'turn'` or `'compression'`, carried onto each attempt record.
- **`ProviderRequestOptions.onSend`** (`SendReport`: `maxTokens`, `reportsContent`) and an argument to **`onProgress`** (`ProgressSeen`: `'content'`). `AnthropicAdapter` and `BedrockAdapter` report the `max_tokens` their body carries, and on `stream()` which events are content. A decorator forwards `onSend` with the other options, and `onProgress` with its argument.
- **`BAD_REQUEST_TEMPLATES`, `REQUEST_FIELD_NAMES` and `classifyBadRequest`**: a 400's kind as a template id, and the field path its message opens with, numbers as `N`, kept only when every other segment is a request field name.
- **`BedrockAdapter.complete()` sets `ProviderResponse.refusalCategory`** from the `stop_details.category` AWS documents on a non-streamed body. Its stream sets none, since AWS's page doesn't document it in the stream's events. An exception or error event inside a Bedrock stream now keeps the provider's error on the error's raw error, as `error`, as an Anthropic SDK error keeps its body.
